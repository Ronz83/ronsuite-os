# PROPOSED PHASED SCOPE

## PHASE 1 — AI Receptionist & Inbound Automation

**Objective:** Eliminate the gap between a new inquiry and a qualified, confirmed booking. Every inbound touchpoint — web, SMS, and phone — is handled systematically from day one.

**Deliverables:**

- **Twilio SMS Integration:** Configure a dedicated business SMS line under your brand. Inbound text messages are routed into your central system and trigger automated response sequences based on message content and intent.
- **Twilio Voice Integration:** Set up inbound call handling with an AI-assisted receptionist layer. Callers receive a professional, on-brand experience with routing logic based on call purpose (booking, inquiry, support).
- **n8n Webhook Automation — FAQ Engine:** Build and deploy a custom FAQ automation map using n8n webhooks. When a prospect texts or submits a form with a common question (pricing, availability, services, location), the system responds instantly with the correct, pre-approved answer — no staff required.
- **Booking Calendar Embed & Automation:** Integrate your scheduling calendar directly into the inbound flow. Prospects who qualify through the AI Receptionist are prompted to book in real time. Calendar availability is synced and updated automatically.
- **SMS Confirmation Sequences:** Upon booking confirmation, an automated SMS sequence fires immediately — delivering appointment details, prep instructions if applicable, and a reminder sequence at 24 hours and 1 hour prior to the appointment.
- **Email Confirmation Sequences:** Parallel email confirmations are sent alongside SMS, formatted professionally and branded to Test Client LLC.
- **Inbound Lead Notification:** Internal alerts notify the appropriate team member the moment a new lead enters the system or a booking is confirmed.

**Phase 1 Outcome:** Test Client LLC has a fully operational AI Receptionist that captures, responds to, and books inbound leads around the clock — without manual intervention.

## PHASE 2 — Website Build, CRM & Pipeline Configuration

**Objective:** Give Test Client LLC a high-converting digital front-end and a fully organized back-end CRM that tracks every lead from first contact to closed deal.

**Deliverables:**

- **Multi-Page Website Build:** Design and develop a professional, brand-aligned website built for conversion. Pages include: Home, Services, About, Contact, and a dedicated Booking landing page. The site is mobile-optimized, fast-loading, and wired directly into the CRM and AI Receptionist system from Phase 1.
- **Lead Capture Integration:** All website forms, contact buttons, and booking CTAs feed directly into the GoHighLevel CRM pipeline in real time. No lead falls outside the system.
- **Central Client Communications Inbox:** Configure the unified GHL inbox as the single source of truth for all client-facing communication — SMS, email, web chat, and form submissions consolidated into one view for your team.
- **Custom Sales Pipeline Setup:** Build a tailored pipeline inside GHL that reflects Test Client LLC's actual sales stages — from first inquiry through to closed and fulfilled. Pipeline stages are named, sequenced, and configured to trigger automations at each transition.
- **Custom Database Parameters & Contact Fields:** Map and configure all custom data fields relevant to Test Client LLC's business — service type, lead source, appointment status, follow-up stage, and any business-specific data points identified during co-discovery.
- **Lead Source Tracking:** Tag and track where every lead originates (website form, SMS, referral, directory, etc.) so Test Client LLC has clear visibility into what channels are working.
- **Pipeline Automation Triggers:** Configure automated actions that fire when a contact moves between pipeline stages — including follow-up SMS sequences, internal task assignments, and status updates.

**Phase 2 Outcome:** Test Client LLC has a professional digital presence that generates leads and a fully configured CRM that organizes, tracks, and follows up with every one of them — automatically.

## PHASE 3 — Launch, Training & White-Glove Handover

**Objective:** Bring the complete system live, ensure the team is confident operating it, and hand over full ownership with nothing left ambiguous.

**Deliverables:**

- **Pre-Launch System Audit:** Full end-to-end test of every automation, webhook, form, calendar, and pipeline trigger before go-live. No component is activated until it has been verified under real conditions.
- **Legacy Data Migration:** Transfer any existing contact records, customer data, or operational information from prior software or spreadsheets into the new GHL CRM. Data is cleaned, formatted, and mapped to the correct custom fields during import.
- **Go-Live Activation:** Flip the system live — DNS propagation, Twilio number activation, automation sequences armed, and pipeline open for real inbound traffic.
- **Team Training Session:** Conduct a structured walkthrough session with the Test Client LLC team covering: how to manage the inbox, how to read and move contacts through the pipeline, how to review automation performance, and how to handle edge cases the AI Receptionist escalates to a human.
- **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Documentation:** Deliver a written SOP document covering the core workflows of the system — so any new team member can be onboarded without requiring NWS involvement.
- **Full Credential Handover:** Deliver a complete, organized handover of all system credentials, login access, domain records, and integration keys relevant to Test Client LLC's build.
- **30-Day Post-Launch Check-In:** Schedule a follow-up review call 30 days after go-live to assess system performance, address any friction points, and identify opportunities for optimization or expansion.

**Phase 3 Outcome:** Test Client LLC's system is live, the team is trained, all data is migrated, and full operational ownership has been transferred. Ongoing support continues under the NWS Platform Subscription.
