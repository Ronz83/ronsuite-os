# NWS Proposal: Digital Architecture for Apex Hauling & Construction

## Executive Summary

Apex Hauling & Construction does not need another website that sits idle. You need a functional operating system that captures every inbound lead, answers customer questions around the clock, and books appointments without manual intervention.

During our co-discovery session, three critical operational bottlenecks emerged: the absence of a high-converting digital presence, the lack of a centralized command center for managing leads and client communications, and the costly reality of missed calls and unanswered inquiries leaking revenue out of your pipeline.

This proposal solves all three. We will architect and deploy a custom high-converting website, a centralized GoHighLevel CRM that serves as your single source of truth, and an n8n-powered Twilio AI Receptionist engineered to answer FAQs and automate your appointment booking — capturing demand the moment it arrives, day or night.

We build functional systems, not static websites. The result is an automated lead-capture, customer support, and scheduling pipeline that works as hard as you do.

## Proposed Phased Scope

### Phase 1: Inbound & Scheduling Automation (AI Receptionist)
- Deploy Twilio SMS and Phone system integrations to capture and route every inbound contact.
- Configure n8n webhook automations to answer custom hauling and construction FAQs (service areas, equipment availability, quote requests, scheduling windows).
- Build a custom high-converting website engineered to convert traffic into booked jobs and qualified leads.
- Embed automated booking calendars with instant SMS/Email confirmation and reminder sequences to reduce no-shows.

### Phase 2: CRM & Pipeline Setup (GHL)
- Configure the central GoHighLevel client communications inbox — unifying SMS, email, and call records in a single command center.
- Set up a customized sales pipeline tracking lead statuses from first contact to closed job.
- Map custom database parameters tailored to Apex operations (job type, site location, project value, equipment requirements).
- Connect website lead-capture forms and AI Receptionist bookings directly into the CRM for seamless, zero-leak intake.

### Phase 3: Launch & White-Glove Support
- Complete database migration from any legacy software or manual records into the GHL system of record.
- Conduct a hands-on team training session covering system operations, pipeline management, and AI Receptionist workflows.
- Handover of complete system credentials and documentation.
- Activate Extendly white-glove support (branded as NWS) for ongoing operational assistance.
